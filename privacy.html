Privacy Policy

Realm of Chaos — therealmsofchaos.com · last updated 31 October 2026

This policy covers the membership portal at therealmsofchaos.com and the internal tools that keep it in step with our Patreon page. It is written to be read, not to be impressive.

What we hold

  • Your email address. It is your key to the vault. You enter it to unlock the material your membership tier covers.
  • Your Patreon membership state — the tier you are on, whether it is currently active, and the dates Patreon shows us. We take this from Patreon's own systems; we do not ask you for it twice.
  • Nothing else. No payment details reach us. Patreon processes every payment. We never see a card, a bank account, or a billing address.

Why we hold it

For one reason: to decide which vault you may enter. Nothing else. We do not build profiles, we do not run ads, and we do not sell, rent or trade your details to anyone.

Who can see it

Only the operator of Realm of Chaos, and the automated tooling on the machine that runs this portal. Our membership database is not published, is excluded from our public code repository, and returns 404 to anyone who requests it from the web.

Where it lives

Membership records sit in a private spreadsheet owned by the operator, and reference copies stay on the operator's own machine. Access credentials for the services we use (Patreon, Google, Make) are stored locally and are never shared or published.

How long

While you are a member. If you cancel, your record moves to an inactive state and stops granting access. You can ask us to delete it entirely.

Your choices

Email goddesserisofchaos868@gmail.com to ask what we hold about you or to have it deleted. We will action a deletion request within 30 days. Cancelling on Patreon is done through Patreon, not through us.

Age

This site is for adults only. We do not knowingly hold data for anyone under 18. Tell us and we will remove it.
